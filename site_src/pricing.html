Billing questions

How do I cancel?

Open the app, click Manage billing next to your name, and cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, within 14 days of your first payment if FutureX isn't for you. See the refund policy.

Who processes my payment?

Lemon Squeezy is our merchant of record. They handle the card payment, receipts, and sales tax/VAT. FutureX never sees your card number.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Change plans or interval anytime from Manage billing; the difference is prorated.

Is this financial advice?

No. FutureX is an analysis and education tool. It doesn't tell you what to trade and doesn't guarantee any outcome. Read the risk disclosure before trading with leverage.
