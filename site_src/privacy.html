Market data requests

Your browser fetches public market data directly from CoinGecko, OKX (including a WebSocket connection for live prices), alternative.me and CryptoCompare. Those services see your IP address as with any website request; we don't log these requests.

Storage in your browser

We use localStorage (not cookies) to remember your alert settings, cached market data, whether you've seen the intro, and any referral code you arrived with. Firebase sign-in stores its session in your browser.

  • Subscription and billing records: as long as required for tax and accounting law (typically 6–10 years), held by Lemon Squeezy and in our records.
