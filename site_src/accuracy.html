Every week we replay the past year of daily prices for all 15 coins. At each day we produce the same 7-day forecast the app shows using only data available up to that day, then compare it to what actually happened. Nothing is tuned after the fact.

How to read this. A direction hit rate near 50% means the forecast called up vs down no better than a coin flip. If our error (MAPE) is not lower than the naive baseline ("the price in 7 days equals today's price"), the forecast adds little. We show both so you can judge. Band coverage is how often the real price landed inside the shaded uncertainty band.

Methodology

  • Data: daily closing prices (UTC) from CoinGecko for the past 365 days.
  • Walk-forward: for each origin day t (starting after a 60-day warm-up), the model sees prices up to t only and forecasts t+7.
  • Statistical model: Holt double-exponential smoothing (α = 0.35, β = 0.08), exactly the code in the app (js/core.js, mirrored in scripts/backtest.py).
  • ML model: when the model server is online, Amazon Chronos-Bolt-Small is evaluated on a weekly sample of origins (it's slower to run).
  • Metrics: direction hit rate = share of forecasts whose predicted 7-day direction matched the actual one; MAPE = mean absolute percentage error of the day-7 price; band coverage = share of actual prices inside the forecast band.
  • The full code is open source in our repository: scripts/backtest.py.

Past accuracy does not guarantee future accuracy. See the risk disclosure.
