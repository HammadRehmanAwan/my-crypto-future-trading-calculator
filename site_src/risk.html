Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Please read this before trading with leverage.

Leveraged crypto futures are high-risk. Most retail traders lose money. You can lose your entire margin within minutes. Only trade with money you can afford to lose completely.

Leverage magnifies losses

With 10× leverage, a 10% move against you wipes out your margin. In practice it's sooner: exchanges liquidate before your margin reaches zero (the maintenance margin). With a 0.5% maintenance margin, a 10× position is liquidated after roughly a 9.5% adverse move, a 50× position after about 1.5%, and a 100× position after about 0.5%. Crypto routinely moves that much within an hour.

Liquidation can happen at a worse price

In fast markets, liquidation engines may fill at prices worse than the calculated liquidation price, and exchanges may charge liquidation fees. Tiered maintenance margins mean large positions are liquidated sooner. FutureX's calculator uses standard formulas and default assumptions. Your exchange's actual numbers may differ, so always confirm on the exchange.

Funding and fees add up

Perpetual futures charge funding (typically every 8 hours) and trading fees. On leveraged positions held for days, these can turn a winning trade into a losing one. FutureX estimates them, but rates change constantly.

Forecasts and signals are not predictions you can rely on

  • Short-term crypto price moves are dominated by noise and news. No model, including ours, predicts them reliably.
  • FutureX's forecasts are statistical (Holt exponential smoothing) and machine-learning estimates (e.g. Amazon Chronos-Bolt, XGBoost). They are trained on or fitted to past prices, and past performance does not predict future results.
  • We publish our measured forecast accuracy on the accuracy page, including periods where forecasts did no better than assuming the price stays the same.
  • Scores, verdicts and "probabilities" summarise indicators; they are not the probability that a trade will win.
  • AI copilot answers are generated text and can be confidently wrong.

Market, platform and regulatory risks

  • Exchanges can halt trading, change rules, fail or become insolvent.
  • Data feeds can be delayed or wrong; alerts can arrive late or not at all. Don't rely on FutureX alerts as your only risk control. Use stop-losses on your exchange.
  • Crypto derivatives are restricted or prohibited in some countries (for example, retail crypto derivatives are banned in the UK). It's your responsibility to comply with the laws where you live.
  • Profits may be taxable.
