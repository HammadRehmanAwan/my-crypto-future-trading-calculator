Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Cancel anytime

You can cancel a Pro or Trader subscription at any time from Manage billing in the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep full access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

14-day money-back guarantee

If FutureX isn't right for you, request a refund within 14 days of your first payment on a plan and we'll refund it in full, no questions asked. This applies once per customer.

Renewals

Renewal payments are generally non-refundable, because you can cancel before a renewal at any time. If you were charged for a renewal you didn't intend (for example, you forgot to cancel a yearly plan), contact us within 7 days of the charge and, provided you haven't meaningfully used the paid features since, we'll refund it.

Service problems

If a paid feature was unavailable for an extended period due to a problem on our side, contact us and we'll provide a prorated refund or credit.

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account with your order number (from your Lemon Squeezy receipt). Refunds are issued by our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy, to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't affect any statutory rights you have as a consumer where you live, including any withdrawal right that applies to digital services.
